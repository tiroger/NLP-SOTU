A short guide to the labels

Negative expresses harmful conditions, criticism, loss, worry, or disapproval.

Neutral contains no clear positive or negative sentiment.

Positive expresses favorable conditions, praise, hope, gratitude, or approval.

Ambiguous mixes sentiments without a clear direction, or needs more context.

Judge the words, not whether you agree with the policy or speaker. A positive response to a harmful event may still describe negative conditions. Use “Ambiguous” when the sentence alone does not support a confident choice.
